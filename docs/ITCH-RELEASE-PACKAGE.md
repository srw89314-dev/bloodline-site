# itch.io Release Package: Bloodline v0.9.0

Nothing in this file is published automatically.

## Devlog Title

Bloodline v0.9.0: Your Family Legacy Matters More

## Devlog Body

Bloodline's next update is focused on making the game we already have stronger before adding another era.

The biggest change is the handoff between generations. Your heir now receives more than part of the estate: they carry a defining trait, a family-reputation bonus, and a remembered highlight from their parent's life. The Family tab shows exactly what crossed into the new generation.

I also tightened the historical-content rules across actions, random events, and storylines. Period-specific restrictions now use one shared system, and the Wild West received another wording pass based on player feedback.

Finally, there is a short three-question survey after a completed life. It does not require an account or cloud save. I am using those answers, along with anonymous progression totals, to decide what should improve next and whether Bloodline is ready for another era.

Please play one complete life, continue as an heir, and tell me:

- Did the era feel believable?
- Did you want to continue the bloodline?
- What should improve first?

Thank you for helping shape Bloodline while it is still early.

## Short Release Summary

Inheritance now carries a defining trait, family reputation, and a remembered life highlight into the next generation. This update also strengthens era authenticity, adds anonymous progression measurement, and introduces a three-question post-life survey.
