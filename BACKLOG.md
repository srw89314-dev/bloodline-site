## Expansion Gate

Do not choose the next era from instinct alone. Review after at least 25 new completed lives or 15 post-life pulse responses, whichever comes first.

Add another era only when:

- At least 35% of tracked players complete a first life.
- At least 50% of players who complete a life continue as an heir.
- Fewer than 20% of post-life responses say the era was not believable.
- No unresolved era-authenticity bug affects a core action or recurring event.
- "More eras" is the most-selected improvement priority, or written feedback repeatedly requests the same era.

## Next Decisions After Evidence

- Rank requested eras by demand, distinct gameplay possibilities, and research cost.
- Prototype one era with a small event set before committing to a full release.
